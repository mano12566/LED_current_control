LED Current Control Using Ohm's Law
About the Project
This is my first electrical engineering simulation project. I used the Falstad Circuit Simulator to build a simple LED circuit and understand how voltage, resistance, and current are related.
Objective
The main goal of this project is to understand Ohm's Law and see how changing the resistance affects the current in a circuit.
Components
9 V DC source
900 Ω resistor
Red LED
Falstad Circuit Simulator
Circuit

The circuit contains a 9 V source, a 900 Ω resistor, and a red LED connected in series.
Ohm's Law
The formula I used is:
I = V / R
For example:
V = 9 V
R = 900 Ω
So:
I = 9 / 900 = 0.01 A = 10 mA
This gives a theoretical current of 10 mA using the simple Ohm's Law calculation.
Experiment
I kept the voltage at 9 V and changed the resistance to see how the current changes.

This helped me understand the relationship between voltage, resistance, and current in a practical way.
